Sand Central Station

Full story and 100% mission walkthrough

Completion requirements: Finish the story, achieve True Believer, collect all 10 minikits and rescue Stan Lee. These award four Gold Bricks in total: one for each requirement, with one brick for the complete minikit set. All ten minikits also complete this level's comic.

Before you begin

This guide covers the original LEGO Marvel Super Heroes console/PC campaign. Play the story route first, then return in Free Play for a complete collection run. Finish the campaign and unlock or purchase the additional characters you need before attempting every detour. The numbered minikits below identify this guide's route, not a separate numbering system shown by the game.

Free Play characters and abilities: Iron Man Mark 42 (flight, missiles and heat beam), Hulk (strength), Wolverine (digging and claw switches), Thor (electricity), Spider-Man (senses and webs), Jean Grey (telekinesis and mind control), Sandman (sand panels) and Iceman (freezing).

Route warning: Finish the four street collectibles before fighting Abomination. Finish the terminal before moving onto the roof; start the five-vent challenge as soon as you arrive there.

Reaching True Believer

Sweep both pavements before entering the station, including behind the left-hand columns. Break the street furniture, collect the studs released by the terminal puzzles, and check the roof edges before finishing Sandman.

You need 70,000 studs in a single visit to this mission; totals from separate attempts do not add together. Smash scenery, follow stud trails and recover dropped studs promptly after a death.

Enabled stud-multiplier extras count towards the meter: with only x2 active, 35,000 ordinary stud value reaches this target. Check that the True Believer banner has appeared before ending or saving your run. You can earn it in Story Mode or Free Play.

Full story route

Team: Iron Man, Hulk and, on the roof, Spider-Man.

Goal: Break into Grand Central and defeat Abomination and Sandman.

1. Clear the street

The opening mission teaches the game's most useful habit: match the obstacle to the character before attacking. Iron Man handles silver LEGO with targeted missiles; Hulk deals with green handles and heavy cracked surfaces. Use the on-screen action names and prompts, since button labels vary between platforms.

Move along the road until the sand-covered vehicle blocks your progress. Aim Iron Man's missiles at the silver fittings on the two nearby hydrants. The water exposes a cracked section for Hulk. Smash it, grab the newly accessible green handles, and repeatedly press the prompted button to pull the vehicle apart. Throw the piece away to clear the road.

At the next large vehicle, destroy its silver section with Iron Man. Build the loose pieces into green handles using a small character, then switch back to Hulk and pull. If Hulk cannot assemble a pile, use Iron Man or transform Hulk into Banner first. Continue to Abomination, engage with Hulk, and follow the changing combat prompts until the fight takes you into the terminal.

2. Break through the terminal

Sandman's enormous arms block the concourse. On the right, missile the silver pipes to uncover a heavy object. Hulk can lift it and throw it at an arm. Use the other green-handled object on the left against the remaining arm. Aim at the obstruction rather than throwing blindly down the room.

Advance until a sand train deposits a crowd of enemies. This is a combat gate: defeat the number shown on the screen and the train dissolves. The counter is your reliable guide; endlessly attacking the scenery will not open this section.

At the information kiosk, destroy the silver assembly above it. Build the resulting water cannon, operate it, and sweep the stream across the sand barrier until the green cracks appear. Hulk can then smash through. At the sandcastle beyond, destroy the silver framework on the right, build the heavy projectile, and throw it into the cracked gate with Hulk. This opens the route to the roof.

3. Bring down the rooftop barrier

Spider-Man joins here. The electric barrier is powered from both sides, so deal with both generators before trying to cross. On the left, Iron Man removes the silver obstruction; Spider-Man's senses reveal a concealed web attachment. Pull it to disable that side.

On the right, use Spider-Sense at the marked wall and climb. Pull the attachment above to bring down the rooftop structure. Assemble its debris into the green-handled mechanism below, then use Hulk to wreck the second generator. With the electricity gone, have Spider-Man pull the large letter A down to make a bridge.

After each animation, inspect the same area for new pieces or handles. Spider-Sense must reveal some targets before webbing can interact with them.

4. Sandman: water cannon and mixer

Cross into the boss area and keep moving between tasks. Sandman is too large to defeat with ordinary attacks while he is standing. Your objective is to create openings and then strike his exposed green-cracked section with Hulk.

Use Spider-Man on the attachment beside the right-hand tower to create a climbing rope. Transform Hulk into Banner, climb, then transform back at the top. Pull the green handles to break the tank assembly and obtain the parts for a water cannon. Build it with a small character and spray Sandman. When he slumps forward, switch to Hulk and hit the cracked part.

For the next opening, find the platform exposed on the left after Sandman's attack. Hulk's jump-and-smash breaks it apart. Assemble the pieces into a cement mixer, grab its green handles with Hulk, aim at Sandman and throw. When the impact exposes another cracked section, move in and smash it. Do the finishing hit promptly rather than continuing to fight the small sand enemies.

5. Sandman: release the final tank

The last phase needs two controls. Spider-Man pulls the cover from the left-hand control; Iron Man destroys the silver cover on the right. Operate both levers. Next, use Spider-Sense by the water tank to reveal its web attachment and pull it. The released water creates the final opening for Hulk's attack, ending the mission.

Complete Free Play collection route

Follow these entries alongside the story route above. Complete each area's detours before taking its exit. For a challenge involving several objects, start at the first listed location and finish the whole set in the same replay. A challenge's reward may appear later than another numbered minikit.

Minikit 1: Street — cracked billboard

From the raised road, face the cracked billboard on the left. Have Hulk throw a car or a piece of ground into it. Switch to a flying character and enter the opening behind the broken sign to collect the canister.

Minikit 2: Street — three blue mailboxes

After clearing the bus, explore the lower pavements. Smash the two blue mailboxes on the left, one towards each end, then the third on the right by the shops. The final mailbox releases the minikit. These can be destroyed with the story team.

Minikit 3: Street — comic shop

On the right-hand lower pavement, find the electrical unit beside NYC Comics. Charge it with Thor, then use Spider-Man's senses to reveal the door handles. Pull both with webs and enter the opened shop.

Minikit 4: Street — pizza delivery

Near the front-right of the street is a dig patch. Use Wolverine to uncover the scooter and assemble it if necessary. Mount it and follow the stud trail to the pizza stand. Stay on the vehicle until the delivery awards the minikit.

Minikit 5: Terminal — hanging gold object

Fly to the left balcony. Use Mark 42's heat beam on the gold fixture supporting the hanging cage/chandelier. Let it fall and break, then descend to collect the released minikit. Rescue Stan on this same balcony before moving on.

Stan Lee in Peril

Inside the terminal, fly onto the upper balcony on the left. Smash the green newspaper/vending machine pinning Stan down. Wait for his rescue confirmation. This rescue is possible with Iron Man in Story Mode.

Minikit 6: Terminal — Sandman excavation

To the right of the information kiosk, use Spider-Sense to reveal the pipe's attachment/valve. Open it and melt the exposed gold with Mark 42. Use Sandman on the resulting sand panel; his digging transformation uncovers the collectible.

Minikit 7: Terminal — sealed side room

Beyond the water-cannon wall, cut open the gold doorway on the left. Activate the claw switch with Wolverine. Use Jean Grey to control the civilian behind the glass and walk that civilian into the minikit; your hero cannot simply walk through the window.

Minikit 8: Roof — clock statues

At the front edge of the first rooftop, find the clock and its purple-highlighted statues. Use Jean Grey's telekinesis on the assembly. Allow the musical animation to finish and collect the reward.

Minikit 9: Roof — five gold vents

Melt all five vents with Mark 42: one on the left of the initial roof, one atop the small right-hand structure, then three beyond the fallen A sign. In that second area, check the left edge by the crossing, the far-left side and the right water-tower structure.

Minikit 10: Roof — ice slide

During the first Sandman phase, break the right-hand water tower and build the story water cannon. Freeze the puddle beside it with Iceman. Ride the resulting slide all the way down to collect its minikit before completing the boss fight.

Final completion check

  • Confirm 10/10 minikits.
  • Confirm the Stan Lee rescue message.
  • Confirm the 70,000-stud True Believer target has registered, either on this run or an earlier saved run.
  • Finish the mission if you still need its story-completion Gold Brick.

If the story is already complete, use the pause menu's exit/return option and choose Save and Exit to keep new collectibles. Wait for saving to finish. Previously collected minikits are replaced by studs on later visits, so check the saved counter instead of expecting every canister to remain visible. If a multi-object challenge is incomplete, finish it before leaving.

These checks complete this story mission. There are no Deadpool red bricks or separate Free Play character-token hunts in these 15 main story levels. Bonus missions, Manhattan activities, character purchases and other hub collectibles are separate requirements for 100% of the entire game.
