Full story walkthrough and 100% completion guide

This guide covers Sand Central Station in LEGO Marvel Super Heroes. Follow the story walkthrough, work towards the True Believer target, collect all 10 minikits and rescue Stan Lee.

Level target: 70,000 studs for True Believer.

Introduction

Sand Central Station is an early story level in LEGO Marvel Super Heroes. This page brings the main level route and optional collectibles together in one place.

If you are aiming for 100% completion, do not focus only on finishing the story. Keep track of your studs, minikits and the Stan Lee rescue as you work through the level.

The True Believer target for this level is 70,000 studs. Collect studs throughout the level to work towards the target.

Story Walkthrough

This section follows the main story progression. Explore the areas you pass through and break objects whenever possible to collect studs.

Starting the level

Begin by following the level's main route. Clear obstacles and enemies in your way, and look around each area before moving on. LEGO objects can often be destroyed for studs or used as part of a puzzle.

Progressing through the station

Continue through the station by following the game's prompts and using the abilities available to your characters. When progress appears blocked, look for objects that can be broken, switches that can be activated or structures that can be interacted with.

Collect studs as you go. The True Believer target is 70,000 studs, so taking the time to explore can help you reach it before the end of the level.

Keep an eye out for collectibles

Some collectibles are easy to miss if you move straight through the story. Check side areas and look for anything that seems out of the way. If you cannot reach a collectible during the story, make a note of it and return when you have the necessary character or ability.

Finishing the story

Continue following the level objectives until the story section is complete. Before leaving, check the level results to see whether you reached True Believer and how many collectibles you have found.

Completing the story does not necessarily mean the level is fully completed. Use the Extra section below to check your remaining objectives.

Extra and Collectibles

This section is for the optional objectives needed to work towards full completion of Sand Central Station.

True Believer — 70,000 studs

The True Believer target is 70,000 studs. Smash breakable objects, collect studs along the route and explore areas rather than rushing past them.

If you finish below the target, replay the level and focus on collecting more studs throughout the story.

All 10 minikits

There are 10 minikits to collect in the level. Keep track of each one as you find it.

Stan Lee rescue

Stan Lee also needs to be rescued for the level's optional objectives. Check the surrounding area carefully and follow the relevant interaction or character ability needed to complete the rescue.

If you finish the level without rescuing him, replay the level and make the rescue part of your checklist.
