LEGO Marvel Super Heroes: Sand Central Station — 100% Walkthrough

By Anonymous

Tuesday, October 27, 2026

Sand Central Station is the first level of LEGO Marvel Super Heroes. This walkthrough follows the story from the opening street encounter through the station and the final rooftop battle with Sandman. The collectible guide appears after the main walkthrough.

The level's main playable heroes are Iron Man, Hulk, and Spider-Man. Use their different abilities to clear obstacles, defeat enemies, and reach the end of the level. Collect studs as you go; the True Believer target is 70,000 studs.

* * *

Walkthrough: The Street and Abomination

Start by moving through the street and smashing nearby LEGO objects for studs. Use Iron Man's repulsors and beam abilities on suitable targets, and switch to Hulk when you encounter heavy objects that need to be smashed. Build any LEGO pieces that appear when the game indicates that they are needed to proceed.

Continue along the street until Abomination confronts you. Attack him until the game prompts you to perform a character interaction, then follow the on-screen button instructions. Repeat this pattern as the fight progresses, keeping clear of his attacks and using the prompted interactions to move the encounter forward.

After the fight, clear the remaining obstacles and follow the route into Sand Central Station. Smash objects along the way for extra studs before moving on.

* * *

Walkthrough: Inside the Station

Sandman's giant sand hands block the route ahead. Attack the targets available in the area and use the characters' abilities when prompted. Break nearby objects to uncover LEGO pieces, then assemble them when the game indicates that they are part of the solution.

Deal with the sand minions that emerge around the train cars. Keep clearing enemies and destructible objects, and look for pieces that can be assembled into equipment. Switch characters whenever an obstacle requires a different ability.

Find the silver LEGO object near the station area and destroy it with the appropriate Iron Man ability. Build the pieces that appear to create the water cannon. Use the cannon against Sandman when the game gives you control of it; the water weakens his sand form and opens the route forward.

* * *

Walkthrough: Reaching the Rooftop

Follow the route opened by the water cannon and continue through the station. Use Iron Man to fly or deal with beam targets, Hulk to smash heavy obstacles, and Spider-Man to interact with web handles or objects revealed by Spider-Sense. Collect studs on the way and keep following the level markers.

Continue upward until you reach the rooftop, where the final encounter with Sandman takes place. Before progressing, smash accessible objects in the area and collect any studs that are easy to reach.

* * *

Walkthrough: Final Sandman Boss Fight

Attack Sandman when his weak points are exposed, and use the water equipment available in the arena to weaken him. When the objective changes, stop attacking and look for highlighted environmental objects that can be broken or assembled.

Smash the required rooftop objects and build the pieces they leave behind. Use the resulting machinery against Sandman when prompted. You may need to switch characters to reach, activate, or destroy particular objects.

Repeat the pattern of weakening Sandman and interacting with the environment until the final sequence begins. Follow the on-screen prompts to finish the encounter and complete the story section of the level.

* * *

All 10 Minikit Locations

The Minikits are listed below, after the main walkthrough. Some require powers that may not be available during the initial story run, so return in Free Play with the appropriate characters if you cannot collect them straight away.

Minikit 1: Billboard

In the opening street section, find the billboard and throw a nearby vehicle or other throwable object at it. The impact releases the first Minikit.

Minikit 2: Three mailboxes

Search the street for three mailboxes and destroy all of them. Check both sides of the area so you do not miss one. The Minikit appears after all three have been smashed.

Minikit 3: NYC Comics

Find the electrical panel near the NYC Comics storefront and activate it with an electricity character. Use Spider-Man's Spider-Sense to reveal the handles, then pull them to uncover the Minikit.

Minikit 4: Scooter ride

Find a dig site in the street and use a digging character to uncover a scooter. Ride it along the trail of studs to reach the Minikit.

Minikit 5: Golden counterweight

On the first balcony, look for a golden object or counterweight. Use a heat-beam character to melt it and reveal the Minikit.

Minikit 6: Hidden sandpit

Use Spider-Man's Spider-Sense to reveal a hidden golden valve, then melt it with a heat-beam character. Wash away the sand with water and use a digging character at the revealed dig site to uncover the Minikit.

Minikit 7: Gold wall

Find the golden wall in the station section and melt it with a heat-beam character. Use telekinesis on the objects behind it to retrieve the Minikit.

Minikit 8: Rooftop statue

On the rooftop, locate the purple LEGO statue. Use a telekinetic character to manipulate it and reveal the Minikit.

Minikit 9: Five golden rooftop objects

Search the rooftop for five golden vents or objects. Melt all five with a heat-beam character to make the Minikit appear.

Minikit 10: Frozen water slide

During the rooftop encounter, use the water cannon to create a puddle. Freeze the water with an ice character, then slide down the frozen path to collect the final Minikit.

* * *

Stan Lee in Peril

Reach the upper-left balcony in the station section. Stan Lee is trapped near a newspaper dispenser. Destroy the dispenser to free him and complete this level's Stan Lee rescue.

True Believer

Collect at least 70,000 studs during the level to reach True Believer. Smash objects, defeat enemies, and collect studs scattered along the route. If you finish below the target, replay the level and explore more thoroughly.

Level Completion Summary

To complete Sand Central Station to 100%, finish the story, collect all ten Minikits, rescue Stan Lee, and earn True Believer by collecting 70,000 studs. If a collectible is inaccessible during the story run, return in Free Play with the character ability it requires.
